GESTURE GUIDE

One finger: point to aim. Pinch thumb and index finger to select the nearest planet. Open palm: release. Closed fist: pause or resume orbits.
How to use: Drag on the solar system to rotate the view. Pinch to zoom. For hand tracking, open this page on an HTTPS GitHub Pages link, tap Enable camera, and allow camera permission. The first launch downloads the hand-tracking model and needs internet access.
Planet sizes and orbital distances are simplified for visibility. Hand tracking depends on lighting, camera quality, and browser performance. The app does not record or upload camera video.
